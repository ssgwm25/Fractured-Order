## Goal

Create a permanent, code-restricted training experience entered with session code `TRAINING2026`. It must open with the approved full 2:28 branded video, continue as a conversational and audio-guided walkthrough, and provide isolated deterministic practice data for the 12 supported participant profiles: Scribe, Facilitator, and Notetaker on Blue, Red, Green, and Industry.

The training plugin must teach the current application rather than preserve stale behavior from the reference prototypes. It must never grant Game Master, White Cell, SME, Observer, or White Cell Support access, and training attempts must never write to live simulation artifacts, evidence, audit exports, participant seats, timers, or operator grants.

## Learning-design contract

Every role path follows the same instructional rhythm:

1. **Orient:** explain the role's purpose in one plain-language mental model.
2. **Show:** present a worked example using the learner's selected team.
3. **Guide:** ask the learner to complete a small action with hints and immediate feedback.
4. **Practice:** remove most hints and require one authentic role action.
5. **Respond:** introduce a deterministic White Cell or teammate event and ask the learner to react appropriately.
6. **Retrieve:** ask a short “what would you do next?” check without showing the answer first.
7. **Reflect:** recap the handoff chain and identify where the learner checks that work landed.

No step may claim success merely because the learner pressed Next. Mastery steps must observe the required training action or an explicitly correct response. Wrong answers receive a concise explanation and another attempt; they do not create punitive scores.
