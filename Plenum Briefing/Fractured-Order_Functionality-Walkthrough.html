Step 1 · Login

Read first

Step 3 · RFIs · Blue, Green, Red, Industry

Who / where

Each team’s Scribe submits from the RFI section; the White Cell Lead answers from the pending queue. This checks the RFI round-trip and category routing for all four teams.

  1. 1 · ScribeOpen RFI.
  2. 2 · CategoryPick a tab.
  3. 3 · SubmitSubmit RFI.
  4. 4 · White CellAnswer the card.
  5. 5 · ReturnAnswer lands by tab.

Each team submits

Submit Request for Information
Team (Scribe) What to submit
Blue Two RFIs on different category tabs, then Submit RFI.
Green Two RFIs on different category tabs.
Red Two RFIs on different category tabs.
Industry Two RFIs on different category tabs.

Using different tabs is deliberate — it exercises the category routing that the check below relies on.

White Cell answers

Respond to RFI
  • Open each incoming RFI in the pending queue and Respond.
  • Confirm each RFI carries the correct source-team badge.
  • Answered RFIs leave the pending queue.

Check: back on each Scribe surface, White Cell’s responses appear separated by category tab — an answer filed under one category shows under that category, not another.

Step 4 · Strategic Orientations

Move 1 gate

A one-time pre-Move-1 input. Each team records via its Scribe, forwards to its Facilitator, and the Facilitator submits to White Cell. Move 1 stays locked until all four artifacts reach White Cell.

  1. 1 · ScribeComplete the team-specific orientation and forecast workflow.
  2. 2 · ForwardSend to Facilitator.
  3. 3 · FacilitatorSubmit to White Cell.
  4. 4 · GateAll four arrive.
  5. 5 · Move 1Advance unlocks.

What each team records

Strategic Orientation
Blue Choose Blue’s orientation, forecast Red’s orientation, and describe what Red is expected to do.
Green Forecast Blue’s orientation, choose Green’s orientation, and describe Green’s strategy given the forecast.
Red Choose and explain Red’s orientation, then forecast Blue, Green (Asian Pacific), and Green (Europe).
Industry Open the Industry Strategic Plan, complete all six pages for Agriculture, Telecommunications, and Biotechnology, record the one shared Blue forecast, and review the consolidated package. Each sector requires a business overview, three risks, Red priorities, partner map and first ambassador target, three strategic priorities, stance, and red line. Industry does not choose an own orientation.
Scribe

Complete every required catalogue choice and narrative, then Forward to Facilitator.

Facilitator

The forwarded artifact appears as a distinct orientation slide; project if desired, then Forward to White Cell.

Confirm across the platform

Functionality check
  • Once a team records its artifact, the Strategic Orientation button becomes unavailable and the action/proposal control becomes the primary green button.
  • The header live tracker stays on Strategic Orientation / Pre-Move 1 until all four reach White Cell, then flips to Move 1 / Internal Deliberation.
  • The White Cell Strategic Orientation queue shows each own orientation, every target forecast, and the applicable rationale, strategy, or expected-action description with a source-team badge.
  • New entries use Orientation & Forecast; legacy selection-only and forecast-only records keep their original labels.
  • White Cell Move 1 advance controls stay blocked until all four arrive, then unlock.

Can the SMEs see and adjudicate this action?

Operator scope check
  • Econ, NI/Escalation, and Diplomacy & Information SMEs each open a role-scoped PLI review console; TSJ and Verba AI SMEs open external handoff queues (copy narrative, mark done).
  • The SME consoles do not render the team-action queue — SMEs cannot see or review team actions.
  • Team-action review (Review Action: Accept as Complete or Send Back for Improvement) stays a White Cell Lead control and records no outcome.
  • Each SME approves or overrides its own PLI outputs (for example, Econ finalizes Macro PLI); the White Cell Lead then sees the finalized PLI read-only.

Check: each SME seat lands on its own PLI / handoff queue with no team-action review control; the White Cell Lead retains action review and sees finalized PLI read-only.

Scribe

Facilitator

Open from the Proposals section (below Actions), project, then Forward to White Cell. It then routes to the intended recipient team.

Recipient response & checks

Proposal response
  • The recipient Facilitator responds with exactly one of Accept, Not Interested, or Negotiate.
  • Accept and Not Interested require confirmation; Negotiate requires terms. The committed response is final and read-only.

Check: White Cell sees the proposal with its source-team badge. On the Green Facilitator surface, the Proposal process panel updates live without a refresh: White Cell review → awaiting recipient response → Accepted / Declined / Negotiation requested (terms shown in full). A decision fires the activity bell / toast exactly once.
