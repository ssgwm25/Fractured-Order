What happens next

Proposal lifecycle
  • White Cell chooses Forward to Blue Team, Request Changes, or Reject Proposal.
  • Forward is a proposal review decision, not an action outcome.
  • Blue may Accept, choose Not Interested, or Negotiate with required terms.
  • The recipient decision is final and visible to Green and White Cell.

Playthrough target: White Cell forwards to Blue; Blue chooses Accept.

White Cell Guide

Operator split

White Cell Lead can review actions, Strategic Orientations, and proposals. Support can inspect queues and assist, but cannot submit reviews. Review all Strategic Orientation artifacts before advancing the Move 1 gate.

  1. OrientationsReview all four team artifacts.
  2. ActionsAccept as complete or return for improvement.
  3. ProposalsApprove each recipient independently or return for changes.
  4. RFIsAnswer from the pending queue.
  5. TimelineVerify each state change.

End-of-loop verification

Live state
  • Accepted actions show Completed; returned actions retain their revision history and submitting-team-specific review notes. Proposals show their review and recipient state separately.
  • Each independently approved Blue or Red recipient receives a separate append-only thread; Accept / Not Interested / Negotiate starts round 1, and later Facilitator replies append without overwriting history.
  • Answered RFIs leave the pending queue, retain priority/categories, and create an RFI_ANSWERED timeline event.
