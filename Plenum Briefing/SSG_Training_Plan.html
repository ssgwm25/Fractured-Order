SSG Platform Training

4-Session Instructional Plan for Undergraduate Cohort

Getting Comfortable with the Plenum Interface

Session 01
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

The Basics

We will go over what the Plenum platform is and the basic layout. You will learn the core interface areas you need to know before using it for the first time.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

Guided Navigation

Everyone gets a role. We will specifically focus on basic navigation: logging in, finding your assigned role's dashboard, locating the main communication channels, and reading a basic brief without fear of breaking things.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Wrapping Up a Turn

Learning how to log out and finish your session safely. We will close the current action and talk about what we just did.

Making Your First Moves on Plenum

Session 02
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

How to Take Action

We will look at how actions work in Plenum and how to submit a decision using a simple 3-step checklist.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

Submitting a Decision

Everyone gets a role. We will specifically focus on the action submission process: drafting a message, selecting an action type from the dropdowns, double-checking the details, and hitting submit to build muscle memory.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Wrapping Up a Turn

We will review the choices we made and how they look in the system. Then, we will close the turn to make our final move.

Tracking Intelligence and Global Metrics

Session 03
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

Following the Info

We will learn how to see the results of your actions. You will learn how to read Intelligence Reports and track global metrics like the Escalation Scale and National Interest markers.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

Reading the Feedback Loop

Everyone gets a role. We will specifically focus on finding where new intelligence updates appear in the feed, monitoring changes to the global board, and tracing a piece of information from start to finish using the platform's tools.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Wrapping Up a Turn

We will talk about what we learned from checking our intelligence feeds. Then, we will close the turn to save our work.

Running a Full Turn in Plenum

Session 04
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

Putting It All Together

We will bring together navigation, making moves, and tracking information into a single, smooth process for a full turn.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

Smooth, Fast-Paced Practice

Everyone gets a role. We will specifically focus on speed and confidence. You will run a mini-game turn from start to finish: reading a brief, submitting an action, checking the intelligence feed for results, and finalizing the move without needing step-by-step guidance.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Final Wrap Up

We will close our last turn. Then, we will have a final chat to make sure everyone is comfortable and ready to join the team!
