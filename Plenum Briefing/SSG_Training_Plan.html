Integrated Training & Playthrough

Complete Instructional Plan for the Fractured Order Undergraduate Cohort
Students can access the tutorial2026 session code at any time for a guided onboarding tour of the platform for each role. For the in-person meeting playthroughs, the session code is training2026. The detailed training plan and playthroughs will all occur through training2026.

Platform Basics & Role Surfaces

Session 01
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

Platform & Roles Overview

We will begin with an overview of the role of Plenum as an operational layer for simulation delivery and an explanation of why we use it. We will cover the core architecture of Fractured Order, and thoroughly define the responsibilities and permissions of the Scribe, Facilitator, and Notetaker roles to ensure smooth gameplay.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

Guided Navigation & Comms

Everyone joins a team (Blue, Red, Green, or Industry). We will practice joining the session, opening your specific role surface, navigating your dashboard, and sending a test Direct Communication.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Wrapping Up a Turn

Learning how to log out safely and ensure your session state is saved.

Playthrough Sequence

Focus: Dashboard Familiarity & Communication

1
White Cell (Game Master) Navigates to the White Cell interface and clicks "Initialize Game". They verify the initial state (Turn 1, Escalation 5) and share the unique 6-letter join code generated by the system on the main projector screen.
2
All Team Scribes Navigate to the Landing page. They input the join code. From the dropdowns, they select their respective teams and choose the "Scribe" role. Upon entering the workspace, they verify that the "Strategic Orientation" button is active.
3
All Team Facilitators Join using the same code, selecting their teams and the "Facilitator" role. They verify their view contains the Team Action Review, Deck, RFIs, and Communications tabs.
4
White Cell Opens the Communications tab in the main White Cell dashboard. They select all teams and send the initial comms check:
"White Cell to all elements. Comms check. Please respond to verify connection."
5
All Team Facilitators See the incoming message in their Communications tab. They reply directly to White Cell:
"Comms check acknowledged from [Team Name]. Standing by."
6
White Cell Verifies receipt of all 4 replies, proving the two-way direct messaging system is functional.
7
Notetakers Join using the code and select the Notetaker role to view the global dashboard.

Session Outcomes

  • Understanding of the Fractured Order structure and team division.
  • Successful login and navigation to role-specific workspaces.
  • Ability to send and receive Direct Communications within the platform.

Strategic Orientation (Pre-Move 1)

Session 02
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

Defining Strategic Orientation

We will discuss the concept of Strategic Orientation as a mandatory gating mechanism before Move 1. We will explain how setting a baseline posture and forecasting adversary actions forces teams to think critically and provides White Cell with a baseline for narrative adjudication.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

Drafting, RFIs & Submission

Scribes will draft their Strategic Orientation matrix and forward it. Facilitators will submit a Request for Information (RFI) to clarify a rule, project the orientation draft, and hit Submit to White Cell.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Wrapping Up the Phase

We will review the choices we made and confirm that White Cell has advanced the phase.

Playthrough Sequence

Focus: Drafting & Submitting Pre-Game Artifacts

1
White Cell Monitors the "Strategic Orientation" progress bar on the White Cell dashboard, currently showing 0/4 teams completed.
2
All Team Scribes Open the Strategic Orientation form and draft their stances.

Blue Scribe: Selects the 'Freedom of Navigation' posture option. No typing required.

Red Scribe: Forecasts Blue's action using the text box:
"Blue will deploy military assets to the Arctic to establish deterrence."
Green Scribe: Forecasts both sides using the text box:
"Both sides will court us for territorial access in the Arctic. Blue leads with security guarantees, Red with cheaper energy. Our leverage peaks if we stay formally uncommitted through Move 2."
Industry Scribe: Selects the 'Hedge across both routes' posture option. No typing required.

All Scribes hit "Forward to Facilitator".
3
Blue Facilitator Checks the "Team Action Review" tab and sees the drafted Orientation artifact. Realizing they don't understand one of the dropdown options, they open the "RFIs" tab.
4
Blue Facilitator Drafts a question, tags it as 'Mechanics', and hits send:
"How many naval assets does Red currently have stationed near the Bering Strait?"
5
White Cell Sees the RFI pop up. They type the answer and hit send:
"Red currently has a destroyer squadron in the area. A blockade is possible but not yet established."
6
Blue Facilitator Sees the answer appear in their RFI history. Satisfied, they project the drafted Orientation on the team's screen for final approval, then click "Submit to White Cell".
7
White Cell Reviews Red's submitted Orientation. Deciding the forecast is too generic, White Cell clicks "Send Back for Improvement", adds a note, and returns it:
"Forecast must specify exactly which region they are targeting and the timeline of the window."
8
Red Scribe & Facilitator Notice the artifact state change to "Returned". The Scribe edits the unlocked text box, adds the specific details, and forwards it:
"Blue will deploy military assets to the Bering Strait to establish deterrence. Our opening is the 48-hour window before their carrier strike group arrives."
The Facilitator quickly reviews and resubmits it.
9
All Team Facilitators Project their finalized Orientations to their teams for consensus, then hit "Submit to White Cell".
10
White Cell Accepts all 4 artifacts in the queue.
11
White Cell As the 4th artifact is accepted, the White Cell dashboard officially clears the pre-game gate. White Cell advances the timeline and formally announces the beginning of Move 1 to the room.

Session Outcomes

  • Mastery of the Pre-Move 1 Strategic Orientation gate requirements.
  • Ability for Scribes to draft and Facilitators to project and submit the artifact.
  • Understanding of how to submit and monitor Requests for Information (RFIs).

Action & Proposal Workflows

Session 03
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

Mechanics of Actions and Proposals

We will break down the taxonomy of simulation moves. We will examine why Blue and Red draft aggressive Actions (using Instruments of Power), while Green and Industry draft collaborative Proposals. We will also discuss the importance of the Scribe-to-Facilitator handoff to ensure moves are coordinated across the team.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

The Handoff Process

Scribes will draft complex actions (selecting Instruments of Power, Supply Chain areas). They forward to the Facilitator, who completes the Coordinated checks before submitting to White Cell.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Wrapping Up a Turn

We will review the differences between Actions and Proposals and confirm they successfully reached White Cell's queue.

Playthrough Sequence

Focus: Complex Form Submission & White Cell Handoffs

1
White Cell Sets a 20-minute timer for Move 1 drafting and announces it to the room.
2
All Team Scribes Scribes begin drafting their primary Actions and Proposals.

Blue Scribe: Clicks "Draft Action", selects Instrument of Power: "Military". Defines Focus Country as "Arctic Circle".
"Deploy a carrier strike group to the Bering Strait to conduct freedom of navigation operations and establish a persistent deterrent against unauthorized boarding of allied vessels."
Red Scribe: Clicks "Draft Action", selects Instrument of Power: "Military". Defines Focus Country as "Arctic Circle".
"Conduct aggressive naval exercises in the Northern Sea Route to demonstrate sovereignty and discourage foreign commercial transit."
Green Scribe: Clicks "Draft Proposal", specifies Intended Partners (Industry) and Focus Sector (Energy).
"Establish a joint intelligence-sharing network with Industry partners to monitor and track unflagged vessels in the Northwest Passage. Notify Blue."
Industry Scribe: Clicks "Draft Proposal", specifies Intended Partners (States) and Focus Sector (Logistics).
"Invest heavily in icebreaker fleet construction to bypass disputed maritime routes entirely, contingent on a five-year stability guarantee."
All Scribes hit "Forward to Facilitator".
3
All Team Facilitators Open Team Action Review and read their Scribe's draft.

Blue & Red Facilitators: In the final checks toolbar, they check "Executive" under Coordinated (as these are military actions) and click "Submit to White Cell".
Green & Industry Facilitators: Review their Proposals, ensure alignment with the team, and click "Submit to White Cell".
4
White Cell Observes the incoming Actions and Proposals populating the White Cell Adjudication Queue on their dashboard.
5
Notetakers Can view all submitted and accepted moves in the global timeline, capturing the broader narrative of the game.

Session Outcomes

  • Ability to differentiate and submit Actions (Blue/Red) and Proposals (Green/Industry).
  • Mastery of the internal Scribe-to-Facilitator handoff process.
  • Understanding of how to execute coordination and engagement checks before final submission.

Full Turn Integration & Feedback Loops

Session 04
0:00 - 0:30 (Introduction)

Feedback Loops & Adjudication

We will focus on the interaction between player teams and the Game Masters. We will discuss the purpose of White Cell Returns as a tool for enforcing realism, how to properly utilize Requests for Information (RFIs) to clarify ambiguity, and how the PLI system adjudicates outcomes.

0:30 - 0:50 (Playthrough)

Smooth, Fast-Paced Practice

White Cell will send an action "Back for Improvement." The Scribe and Facilitator must review the notes, edit the draft, and resubmit rapidly.

0:50 - 1:00 (Closing)

Final Wrap Up

We will close our last turn and ensure everyone feels confident using the Scribe and Facilitator tools for the live exercise!

Playthrough Sequence

Focus: Running a Full Turn from Start to Finish

1
White Cell (Game Master) Formally announces the start of Move 2 and sets a 25-minute timer on the main screen for all teams to draft their moves.
2
The Scribes Teams immediately begin drafting moves.

Blue Action:
"Initiate bilateral diplomatic talks with neutral Arctic Council members to condemn the vessel boarding and impose localized sanctions."
Red Action:
"Conduct aggressive naval exercises in the Northern Sea Route to demonstrate sovereignty and discourage foreign commercial transit."
Green Proposal:
"Propose a new international treaty standardizing search-and-rescue operations across the Arctic to build goodwill."
Industry Proposal:
"Invest heavily in icebreaker fleet construction to bypass disputed maritime routes entirely."
3
Red Scribe & Facilitator Realize they need context on a specific supply chain mechanic before committing to their Action. The Facilitator submits a high-priority RFI to White Cell:
"Do we currently have enough naval assets in the region to support a blockade?"
4
White Cell Monitors the queue, spots the Red RFI, types a clarifying answer, and sends it back. Red proceeds with their draft.
"Yes, you have a Northern Fleet contingent available."
5
Blue Scribe & Facilitator The Scribe finishes their Diplomatic Action and forwards it. The Facilitator projects it, conducts the Coordinated checks, but deliberately omits the required Focus Country field before hitting Submit to White Cell.
6
White Cell Reviews Blue's action. Catching the error, White Cell uses "Send Back for Improvement" with a note:
"Required Focus Country missing. Fix and resubmit."
7
Blue Scribe & Facilitator Execute the rapid edit loop. The Facilitator sees the return and alerts the Scribe. The Scribe edits the draft to add the country:
"Initiate bilateral diplomatic talks with Iceland and Norway to condemn the vessel boarding..."
They forward it, and the Facilitator resubmits.
8
Green & Industry Facilitators Project their completed Proposals to their teams, gain final consensus, and submit them directly to White Cell's queue.
9
White Cell Reviews all 4 submitted Actions and Proposals. Satisfied with the quality, White Cell marks them all as "Accepted as Complete".
10
White Cell Allocates the remaining time in the phase to process the inputs through the PLI backend. Once resolved, they click "Adjudicate Move".
11
All Teams Observe the timeline header advance to Move 3. They immediately open their "Intelligence" feeds to read the new Intelligence Reports generated by the consequences of Move 2.

Session Outcomes

  • Ability to navigate White Cell feedback and returns efficiently.
  • Confidence in editing and resubmitting returned artifacts.
  • Complete readiness to operate the Scribe and Facilitator surfaces independently during a live, fast-paced exercise.
