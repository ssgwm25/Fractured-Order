Plenum Playthrough Guides

Step-by-Step Scenario Scripts for Undergraduate Training Sessions

Session 1: Platform Basics & Role Surfaces

Focus: Dashboard Familiarity & Communication

Introduction

Welcome to Fractured Order on Plenum. In this simulation, participants form teams consisting of a Scribe, a Facilitator, and Notetakers. They are overseen by the White Cell game masters. Our first session ensures everyone knows how to log in and find their workspace.

The Playthrough Round

To begin, the Game Master opens the session and shares the short uppercase join code. Students navigate to the Landing page.

The Scribes Join the session using the code and select their team (Blue, Red, Green, or Industry) and the Scribe role. They verify their workspace loads and that the Strategic Orientation button is the primary green control.
The Facilitators Join the session and select the Facilitator role for their team. They open the Communications workspace and send a test direct message to White Cell.
The Notetakers Join and access their observation interface to practice recording team dynamics and alliance tracking.

The session concludes once all teams confirm they can comfortably locate their starting conditions and workspace features without errors.

Session Outcomes

  • Understanding of the Fractured Order structure and team division.
  • Successful login and navigation to role-specific workspaces.
  • Ability to send and receive Direct Communications within the platform.

Session 2: Strategic Orientation (Pre-Move 1)

Focus: Drafting & Submitting Pre-Game Artifacts

Introduction

Before Move 1 can begin, each team must clear the Strategic Orientation gate. This requires a coordinated handoff between the Scribe and Facilitator.

The Playthrough Round

Blue Scribe Opens the Strategic Orientation form. They choose Blue's orientation, forecast Red's orientation, and describe what Red is expected to do. They click "Forward to Facilitator".
Blue Facilitator Sees the drafted artifact appear. Before submitting, they open the RFIs workspace and submit an RFI to White Cell to clarify the prompt. Once answered, they project the artifact onto the venue screen for team alignment, and select "Forward to White Cell".
Red, Green, & Industry Teams Complete their specific Strategic Orientation matrices (e.g., Red forecasts Blue, Green (Asian Pacific), and Green (Europe)). The Scribes forward to their Facilitators, who submit to White Cell.

The timeline header on everyone's screen reads "Strategic Orientation / Pre-Move 1". Once White Cell receives all four artifacts, the header updates and Move 1 officially begins.

Session Outcomes

  • Mastery of the Pre-Move 1 Strategic Orientation gate requirements.
  • Ability for Scribes to draft and Facilitators to project and submit the artifact.
  • Understanding of how to submit and monitor Requests for Information (RFIs).

Session 3: Action & Proposal Workflows

Focus: Complex Form Submission & White Cell Handoffs

Introduction

It is now Move 1. Teams will practice drafting comprehensive Actions (Blue/Red) and Proposals (Green/Industry) and executing the final checks before submission.

The Playthrough Round

Blue Scribe Drafts a new Action. They select "Diplomacy" under Instrument of Power. They answer "Yes" to the supply chain focus question, which reveals the Supply Chain Area options, where they select "Extraction". They specify the Focus Countries and expected outcomes, then forward the draft.
Blue Facilitator Receives the draft in the Team Action Review. They use the fixed bottom toolbar to answer the final checks: marking "Legislative" and "Executive" under Coordinated, and marking Industry "Yes" under Informed/Engaged. They submit to White Cell.
Green Scribe Drafts a Proposal. They enter Intended Partners, Focus Sectors, and select Blue and Red under intended notifications with a clarifying note. They forward the draft.

Teams successfully execute the drafts and verify that their artifacts are marked as "Submitted to White Cell" and locked for further editing.

Session Outcomes

  • Ability to differentiate and submit Actions (Blue/Red) and Proposals (Green/Industry).
  • Mastery of the internal Scribe-to-Facilitator handoff process.
  • Understanding of how to execute coordination and engagement checks before final submission.

Session 4: Full Turn Integration & Feedback Loops

Focus: Handling White Cell Returns & RFIs

Introduction

During the move, White Cell may require more clarity, or teams may need more information to proceed. This session covers returns, resubmissions, and Requests for Information (RFIs).

The Playthrough Round

White Cell Reviews Blue's action. Finding the outcome description lacking, White Cell selects "Send Back for Improvement", adds a reviewer note, and returns it.
Blue Scribe & Facilitator Notice their action state change to "Returned by White Cell". The Facilitator clicks Edit, reads the White Cell notes in the revision history, adds the required details, and selects "Resubmit to White Cell".
Industry Facilitator Needs clarification on a game mechanic. They open the RFIs workspace, submit a category-tagged question, and monitor the live feed.
White Cell Answers the Industry RFI. The response appears in the Industry Facilitator's History tab.

The session concludes when all teams have successfully handled a return, processed an RFI, and White Cell allocates time to advance to the next move.

Session Outcomes

  • Ability to navigate White Cell feedback and returns efficiently.
  • Confidence in editing and resubmitting returned artifacts.
  • Complete readiness to operate the Scribe and Facilitator surfaces independently during a live, fast-paced exercise.
