# Plenum Platform Explainer Video Script

## Working Title

**Plenum: The Operational Layer for Fractured Order**

## Audience

Statecraft Simulations Group members who will operate, facilitate, support, or analyze Fractured Order.

## Purpose

Explain:

1. What the Plenum platform is.
2. What operational problem it addresses for Fractured Order.
3. How the platform’s roles work together during a live exercise.
4. Why structured session data matters for review and debrief.

## Recommended Format

- **Length:** 75–90 seconds.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9.
- **Resolution:** 1920 × 1080.
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps.
- **Style:** Professional motion graphics with restrained interface animation.
- **Tone:** Analytical, institutional, clear, and confident.
- **Palette:** Charcoal, black, white, deep navy, muted electric blue, and restrained amber.
- **Audio:** Calm, precise voiceover with subtle ambient digital sound design.

## Core Message

> Plenum is the shared operational layer that connects Fractured Order’s participant workspaces with the control-cell tools needed to run, observe, adjudicate, and review a live economic statecraft simulation.

## Production Notes

- Use abstract interface shapes and animated data pathways rather than fully readable fake interfaces.
- Use real screenshots only when they are current, approved, and free of sensitive participant data.
- Avoid military imagery, weapons, soldiers, flags, national stereotypes, and dramatic war visuals.
- Any on-screen text should be added during editing rather than generated inside visual assets.
- If the video must be text-free, replace labels with distinct shapes, colors, icons, and motion patterns.

## Scene 1 — The Simulation Environment

**Duration:** 0:00–0:10

### Visual direction

Begin with an abstract network representing Fractured Order. Four distinct actor nodes appear around a changing central environment. Data streams, decision cards, timelines, and communication pathways move between the nodes.

The visual should suggest economic statecraft, strategic competition, supply chains, and interaction without depicting real countries or military conflict.

### Voiceover

> Fractured Order is a live economic statecraft simulation. Multiple actors interpret a changing strategic environment, make decisions, communicate, and adapt across several phases of play.

### Optional on-screen text

- Fractured Order
- Economic statecraft simulation

---

## Scene 2 — The Coordination Problem

**Duration:** 0:10–0:22

### Visual direction

The network fragments. Separate cards represent chat messages, shared documents, slide decks, timers, decisions, facilitator notes, and outcome records. They move out of alignment, overlap, and become difficult to follow.

Do not show readable text. Use visual differences in shape, color, and motion to communicate fragmentation.

### Voiceover

> A complex simulation can quickly become difficult to coordinate. Decisions, communications, timing, facilitation, adjudication, and observation may be scattered across disconnected tools and informal processes.

> That makes it harder to know what is current, who owns the next action, and how an event changed the course of play.

### Optional on-screen text

- Fragmented information
- Unclear ownership
- Difficult live coordination

---

## Scene 3 — Introducing Plenum

**Duration:** 0:22–0:34

### Visual direction

A central, calm platform layer forms beneath the fragmented elements. The disconnected cards are drawn into the platform and reorganized into a coherent shared session state.

Use a deep navy field with electric-blue connections and small amber signals for decisions or alerts.

### Voiceover

> Plenum addresses this problem by providing a shared browser-based operating layer for the exercise.

> It connects participant workspaces with the control-cell tools needed to run, monitor, document, and review the simulation.

### Optional on-screen text

- Shared operational layer
- One session state

---

## Scene 4 — The Role Network

**Duration:** 0:34–0:52

### Visual direction

Four role nodes appear around the central platform. Each node has a distinct visual behavior:

- Scribe: structured decision cards entering the session record.
- Facilitator: pathways connecting proposals and responses.
- Notetaker: observation markers and timeline annotations.
- White Cell: central control lines, phase markers, and adjudication signals.

A smaller Game Master node appears above or behind the platform, representing session administration and export.

### Voiceover

> Each role has a defined responsibility within the shared session state.

> The Scribe records orientations, forecasts, actions, proposals, responses, and Requests for Information.

> The Facilitator supports team discussion, reviews received proposals, and helps manage interaction.

> The Notetaker captures team dynamics, alliances, turning points, and the decision process behind the outcome.

> White Cell manages timing, phases, updates, review, and adjudication, while the Game Master administers sessions and exports the evidence.

### Optional on-screen text

- Scribe
- Facilitator
- Notetaker
- White Cell
- Game Master

## Scene 5 — From Decision to Consequence

**Duration:** 0:52–1:06

### Visual direction

Animate a decision moving through the platform:

1. Team discussion.
2. Strategic Orientation or forecast.
3. Action or proposal.
4. White Cell review.
5. Adjudication.
6. New information returned to the team.
7. Strategic adaptation.

Use connected motion rather than literal interface text.

### Voiceover

> During Strategic Orientation and Moves One through Three, Plenum links decisions to timing, communication, review, adjudication, and consequences.

> Teams can record what they choose, why they choose it, how they coordinate, and how they adapt when new information arrives.

### Optional on-screen text

- Orient
- Decide
- Communicate
- Adjudicate
- Adapt

## Scene 6 — The Session Record

**Duration:** 1:06–1:18

### Visual direction

The live network gradually resolves into a structured timeline. Decision cards, communication pathways, adjudication markers, and observation notes align into a coherent session history.

Show abstract report pages or data projections appearing at the end. Do not use fake readable text.

### Voiceover

> The result is more than a live interface. It is a structured record of the exercise: what teams believed, what they decided, what information they received, how they interacted, and how outcomes developed over time.

### Optional on-screen text

- Live exercise record
- Decision history
- After-action evidence

---

## Scene 7 — Closing Message

**Duration:** 1:18–1:28

### Visual direction

Return to the complete connected network. The four actor nodes, role surfaces, White Cell, and Game Master remain connected through the central platform. End on a clean, quiet composition with negative space for the final title card.

### Voiceover

> Plenum does not replace strategic judgment or facilitation. It gives Statecraft Simulations Group the operational structure to focus on them.

> It turns a complex exercise into a coordinated, observable, and reviewable simulation.

### Optional closing text

- Plenum
- The operational layer for Fractured Order
- Statecraft Simulations Group

## Animation Direction

### Motion language

- Use smooth, deliberate transitions.
- Begin with scattered motion and gradually introduce order.
- Use connecting lines to show relationships among roles and records.
- Use restrained pulses to indicate updates, submissions, or adjudication.
- Use transitions from fragmentation to coordination as the central visual metaphor.

### Avoid

- Fast gaming-style interface movement.
- Explosions, combat, weapons, or military command imagery.
- Overly bright neon effects.
- Dense dashboards that require viewers to read small text.
- Unapproved logos or live participant information.
- Claims that Plenum automates strategy or replaces White Cell judgment.

## Accuracy Notes

- Plenum is the delivery and operations platform; Fractured Order is the exercise.
- The built-in actor teams are Blue, Red, Green, and Industry.
- Strategic Orientation precedes Moves 1–3 in the documented exercise topology.
- White Cell controls phase progression, review, communications, and adjudication workflow.
- The Game Master manages sessions, participants, and exports.
- PLI supports multi-track adjudication and SME review.
- Exported evidence can include HTML, LaTeX, CSV, JSON, and JSONL projections.
